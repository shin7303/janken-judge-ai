# Known limitations

- A consumer camera cannot measure the instant that one player actually sees the other player’s hand.
- Commit times are estimates from image recognition and are affected by FPS, exposure, latency, and device performance.
- Motion blur, occlusion, lighting, backgrounds, gloves, and crossing hands can reduce recognition quality.
- A closed fist may already be present before PON and needs a special time-estimation rule.
- The app cannot infer intent. It never confirms cheating and is not suitable for official competition or dispute resolution.
